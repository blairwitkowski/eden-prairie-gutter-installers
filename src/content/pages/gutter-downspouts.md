## What's Included

A downspout's job is simple to describe and easy to get wrong: move water from the gutter to a point where it won't come back toward the house. We size and place downspouts based on how much water each gutter section actually carries, not just by putting one at every corner because that's the default.

- Downspout sizing based on the roof area feeding each section
- Placement that follows your lot's actual grading, not a generic layout
- Extensions and splash blocks where a walkout basement or sloped lot needs the water carried further
- Reconnection of loose or disconnected joints during a repair visit

## Why Placement Matters on Sloped Eden Prairie Lots

A lot of homes here, especially around [Riley Lake](/gutter-installation-in-riley-lake-mn/) and the bluff-line lots in [West Eden Prairie](/gutter-installation-in-west-eden-prairie-mn/), have walkout basements built into a slope. Water discharged from an upper downspout on a lot like that can run downhill and accelerate right back toward the lower level if it isn't redirected properly. We've seen that exact pattern turn into moisture along a walkout basement wall when a downspout was placed correctly on paper but wrong for the actual slope of the yard.

## How The Job Goes

We walk the property, not just the roofline, and look at where the ground actually slopes. Then we size and place downspouts to match, adding extensions where the lot needs water carried further from the foundation. If existing downspouts are undersized for the section they're draining, we'll flag that as part of an installation or repair.

## What Affects The Cost

Number of downspouts, length of run (including any underground tie-ins), and whether extensions or splash blocks are needed for your specific grading.
