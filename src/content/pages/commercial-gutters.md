## What's Included

Office and flex buildings in Eden Prairie's business parks mostly drain differently than a house. Instead of a gutter along a pitched roof edge, a lot of them route water through scuppers in the parapet wall or down internal drains through the roof deck. We inspect and service both systems, along with perimeter gutter runs on buildings that do use them.

- Scupper and internal-drain inspection and clearing
- Perimeter gutter repair and installation where applicable
- Debris removal from flat and low-slope roof drainage points
- Coordination with property managers on scheduling and access

## Why The Building Age Matters Here

A lot of the office and flex space in the [Golden Triangle](/commercial-gutters-in-golden-triangle-mn/) and along [City West Parkway](/commercial-gutters-in-city-west-parkway-mn/) went up in the 1980s and 90s. Buildings that age often have original drainage systems that were never sized for current storm patterns, and a clogged scupper on a flat roof doesn't just overflow onto the ground the way a house gutter does. It can pond on the roof deck itself, which is a bigger problem than a wet lawn.

## How The Job Goes

We inspect the drainage points, clear whatever's blocking flow, and check for any signs of ponding or backup on the roof surface. If a scupper or drain needs repair or replacement rather than just clearing, we'll walk you through what that involves before doing anything.

## What Affects The Cost

Roof size, number of drainage points, access (some flat roofs need a lift, others have roof access from inside the building), and whether this is a one-time service or an ongoing maintenance schedule.
