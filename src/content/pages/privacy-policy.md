## Privacy Policy

Eden Prairie Gutter Installers collects the information you provide through our contact and estimate forms, such as your name, phone number, email address, and a description of your gutter needs. We use this information only to respond to your request and schedule an estimate.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information with Big Mike's Gutters when needed to schedule and complete your service request.

If you have questions about this policy, [contact us](/contact/).
