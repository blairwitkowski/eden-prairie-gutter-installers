## Our Connection to Big Mike's Gutters

Eden Prairie Gutter Installers is owned and run by Mike Carroll and is affiliated with Big Mike's Gutters. Our experienced crew has been installing, repairing, and cleaning gutters across the Twin Cities metro for over a decade.

A local crew understands the roof types, the tree cover, and the drainage quirks of the area. Eden Prairie's mix of steep bluff-line estates, older split-entry homes, and low-slope commercial buildings in the Golden Triangle isn't the same job three times. It's three different sizing and pitch decisions.

## No Subcontractors

Every job is run by our own crew. We don't hand your project off to subcontractors. The people who measure your roof are the same team who come back and hang the gutters.

## What we Install

We fabricate and install one-piece, custom-formed gutters on site rather than joining stock lengths, which cuts down on the seams that fail first when Minnesota winters put a gutter through repeated freeze-thaw cycles. We also install and stand behind name-brand gutter guard systems, including **Leaf Sentry**, **Bulldog Gutter Guard**, **Leaf Blaster**, and **XTREME Gutter Guards**, depending on the tree cover and budget you're working with.
