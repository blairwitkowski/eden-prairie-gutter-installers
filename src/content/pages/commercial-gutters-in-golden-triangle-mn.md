## The Twin Cities' Largest Business Park

The Golden Triangle is one of the Twin Cities' largest business parks, nearly 1,000 acres bordered by Interstate 494 and Highways 169 and 212. It's mostly office space, with a growing mix of flex and industrial buildings and, more recently, housing near the light rail station. Optum's corporate campus anchors the district, and the City West Light Rail Station sits at its northern edge.

A lot of the flex and industrial building stock here dates to the 1980s and 90s. The Eden Prairie Business Center at 7615 Golden Triangle Dr, for instance, is a 53,508 square foot flex/industrial building built in 1986 with masonry construction, a fairly representative profile for the district. Buildings from that era run low-slope roofs with scupper or internal-drain systems, and when those systems weren't sized for current storm intensity, even a building that looks well maintained can pond faster than its age would suggest, which leads property managers here to schedule inspections more often than they might for a newer property.

## How Property Managers Here Get Help

We inspect scupper and internal-drain points for debris buildup, look for signs of ponding on flat roof surfaces, and check whether existing drainage is actually keeping up with current storm volume. For properties that do run perimeter gutters, particularly some of the smaller office and flex buildings, we service those with the same attention we'd give a large residential system.

## Why Waiting Makes it Worse

When ponding forms on a low-slope roof and isn't cleared, the added weight and prolonged water exposure accelerate wear on the roof membrane at that exact spot, which leads to a leak inside the building well before anyone sees a problem from the outside. That's the pattern we see most often across older buildings in this district.

## Commercial Gutter Services Property Managers Here Ask For Most

Scupper and internal-drain clearing, ponding inspections, and periodic [commercial gutter](/commercial-gutters/) maintenance across the district's flex, office, and industrial buildings. We coordinate directly with property managers on access and scheduling.

## Buildings And Drainage Realities Across The Golden Triangle

A 1986 flex building and a newer light-rail-adjacent development don't drain the same way, even a few hundred feet apart in the same business park, which is why we inspect each property's actual drainage layout rather than applying one standard to the whole district.
