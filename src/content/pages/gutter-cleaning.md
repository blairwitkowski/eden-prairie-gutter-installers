## What's Included

A cleaning isn't just scooping leaves out of the trough. We clear debris by hand, flush each downspout to make sure water actually exits at the bottom instead of backing up inside the pipe, and take a visual pass over hangers and seams while we're already on the ladder. If something's pulling loose or a seam is starting to split, we'll tell you before it becomes an overflow problem.

- Hand-clearing of leaves, seed pods, and shingle grit
- Downspout flushing to check for internal clogs
- Visual check of hangers, seams, and fascia condition
- A straight answer on whether gutter guards would help your specific tree cover

## Why This Matters More in Some Parts of Eden Prairie Than Others

Tree cover isn't the same across the city. The wooded lots near [Bryant Lake](/gutter-installation-in-bryant-lake-mn/) and the mature canopy in older neighborhoods toward [Northeast Eden Prairie](/gutter-installation-in-northeast-eden-prairie-mn/) drop a lot more Maple seed and Oak leaf debris than a newer subdivision with young trees. Debris that compacts in a gutter trough or at a downspout opening restricts flow fast, and once it does, water starts backing up and finding the path of least resistance, which is usually over the front edge of the gutter and down the siding.

## How The Job Goes

We come out, clear the system by hand rather than power-blasting it (which can push debris into the downspout instead of out of it), and flush each downspout with water to confirm it drains clean. If we spot a sagging section, a split seam, or a hanger that's pulled loose, we'll point it out and give you a straight recommendation, whether that's a quick [repair](/gutter-repair/) or something bigger.

## What Affects The Cost

Roofline length, how many stories, how much debris has built up, and whether gutter guards are already installed (which changes the cleaning method) all factor in. We give a real number after seeing the roof, not before.
