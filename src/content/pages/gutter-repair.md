## What's Included

Most repair calls come down to one of three things: a sagging section where a hanger has pulled loose, a split or leaking seam, or a downspout that's disconnected at a joint. We fix the failed section rather than defaulting to a full replacement, as long as the rest of the system is in good shape.

- Hanger resecuring or replacement
- Seam resealing and refastening
- Downspout reconnection and realignment
- Fascia inspection where a gutter has pulled away from the house

## Why Sections Fail Here

Freeze-thaw cycles are the main culprit across Eden Prairie. Water that pools and refreezes in a gutter section adds real weight and pressure, and over enough winters that works fasteners loose from the fascia. Valley-fed sections on steeper rooflines, like a lot of what we see around [Southwest Eden Prairie](/gutter-installation-in-southwest-eden-prairie-mn/) and the [Riley Lake area](/gutter-installation-in-riley-lake-mn/), take on more volume during a storm than a straight run does, which puts extra stress on the hangers holding that section up.

## How The Job Goes

We inspect the failed section first, not the whole roofline by default. If a hanger has pulled loose, we check whether the fascia underneath is still solid before resecuring, since a repair into rotted wood won't hold. If a seam has split, we clean and reseal it rather than replacing the run. If the damage turns out to be more extensive than the initial call suggested, we'll tell you before doing anything beyond what you asked for.

## What Affects The Cost

How many sections need work, whether the fascia needs repair underneath, and whether the repair is isolated or a sign the whole system is due for [replacement](/gutter-installation/).
