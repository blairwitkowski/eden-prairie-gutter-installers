## What to include

A rough sense of the problem helps: is water overflowing at one spot every storm, is a section sagging or pulled away from the house, or are you just due for a cleaning? If you know roughly how old the current gutters are, that helps too. If you've got a photo, even a phone snapshot from the ground, it helps us come prepared.
