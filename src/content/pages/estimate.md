## What Happens After You Submit

We'll follow up to set a time to come out, measure the roofline, and give you a straight number. If gutter guards make sense for your tree cover, we'll say so. If they don't, we'll say that too. There's no script pushing you to decide on the spot.

## Before You Submit

A rough sense of the problem helps: is water overflowing at one spot every storm, is a section sagging or pulled away from the house, or are you just due for a cleaning? If you've got a photo, even a phone snapshot from the ground, it helps us come prepared.
