## What's Included

A gutter installation starts with us walking the roofline in person, not measuring off a satellite photo. We look at how many roof planes drain into each section, where the valleys concentrate water, and whether the current downspout placement actually clears the foundation. Then we fabricate **one-piece, custom-formed gutters** on site rather than joining together stock lengths.

That distinction matters more here than in a lot of places. Fewer seams means fewer spots for ice to work loose during a Minnesota freeze-thaw cycle, which is the single biggest cause of gutter failure we see across Eden Prairie. A gutter with a factory seam every ten feet is a gutter with ten feet of extra failure points.

- Tear-off and disposal of the old system
- On-site fabrication in 5" or 6" profiles, aluminum, galvalume, or copper
- Hangers spaced and sized for your roof's snow load, not a generic spacing chart
- Downspout count and placement based on where water actually needs to go
- Gutter guard installation if you want it, from [Leaf Sentry, Bulldog, Leaf Blaster, or XTREME](/about/) systems

## How The Job Goes

We start with a free, in-person estimate. If the roofline has valley sections, like a lot of the newer bluff-line homes around [Bearpath](/gutter-installation-in-bearpath-mn/) and [Settlers Ridge](/gutter-installation-in-settlers-ridge-mn/), we size those sections up rather than running the same width the whole way around. That's a judgment call, not a guess. A valley where two or three roof planes meet can carry several times the volume of a straight gutter run during a heavy storm, and undersizing it just moves the overflow problem from your gutters to your foundation.

On installation day, the crew removes the old system, checks the fascia underneath for rot before hanging anything new, and fabricates the gutter on site using a portable machine that forms the material to length. Downspouts get placed where the grading actually carries water away, not just at the corners because that's where they've always gone.

## What Affects The Cost

Roofline length and how many valleys or corners it has, gutter size (5" versus 6"), material choice, the number of downspouts, and whether the fascia underneath needs repair before we can hang anything all factor in. We won't know a real number until we've walked your roof, which is why estimates are free and in person.
