## Why Waiting Makes it Worse

An aging gutter system that's been patched more than once tends to fail at its weakest patch first, not necessarily its oldest section. When one of those patches finally gives out during a heavier storm, water finds the gap and overflows there specifically, which is usually the moment a homeowner decides it's time for a real fix instead of another patch.
