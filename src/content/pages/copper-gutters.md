## What's Included

Copper gutters are fabricated the same way our standard systems are, custom-formed on site to fit your roofline, but in copper instead of aluminum or galvalume. The material costs more and the installation takes more care, particularly at the seams and joints, which are typically soldered rather than sealed.

- Custom fabrication sized to your roofline
- Soldered seams and joints for a durable, weather-tight finish
- Matching or complementary downspouts and hardware
- Guidance on how the natural patina will develop over time

## Why Homeowners Choose Copper

It's mostly about longevity and looks. Copper develops a natural patina over the years that a lot of homeowners in Eden Prairie's more custom-built neighborhoods, particularly the estate-style homes around [Bearpath](/gutter-installation-in-bearpath-mn/) and the newer executive builds in [Settlers Ridge](/gutter-installation-in-settlers-ridge-mn/), specifically want to match a home's overall design. It also tends to outlast standard materials by a wide margin when it's installed and maintained correctly, which matters on a house where you're not planning to redo the exterior again anytime soon.

## How The Job Goes

We measure the same way we would for any installation, then fabricate the copper on site and solder the seams rather than using sealant, which holds up better over the material's long lifespan. Hangers and fasteners are chosen to avoid reacting badly with copper over time, which is a detail that gets missed when a crew doesn't work with the material often.

## What Affects The Cost

Material cost is the biggest factor and runs well above aluminum or galvalume. Roofline length, number of downspouts, and the complexity of soldered joints also factor in.
